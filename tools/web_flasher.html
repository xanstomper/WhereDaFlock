Flashing Instructions:
  1. Connect your ESP32-S3 board to your computer using a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Install on the desired firmware configuration above.
  3. Select your device's USB COM port in the popup browser dialog (e.g. ttyACM0 or usbmodem).
  4. If the device fails to connect, hold down the BOOT button on the board, tap RESET, and release BOOT to enter ROM download mode.
  5. Wait 30 seconds for the flash to finish, then unplug and reconnect the device.
